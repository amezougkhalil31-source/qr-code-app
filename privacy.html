Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 04, 2026

Welcome to QR Master Pro. We are committed to protecting your personal privacy and ensuring a transparent user experience. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application handles your information, camera permissions, advertisements, and premium subscriptions.

1. Information We Collect

QR Master Pro is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not require you to create an account, nor do we collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number directly.

Camera Permission Usage

Our app requests access to your device's Camera solely for the purpose of scanning QR codes and barcodes in real-time. Image and video data processed by the camera are used instantly on your device and are never stored, logged, or transmitted to any external servers.

2. Advertisements

To keep QR Master Pro free for general use while supporting ongoing development, we integrate third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdMob). These advertising partners may collect and use non-personal data (such as device identifiers, IP addresses, and ad interaction metrics) to serve personalized or contextually relevant advertisements.

3. Subscriptions and Premium Services

QR Master Pro offers optional monthly and yearly subscription plans to unlock advanced features and an ad-free experience.

  • Payment Processing: All financial transactions, renewals, and billing details are securely processed through official third-party app stores and billing platforms (such as Google Play Billing).
  • Data Security: We do not store, process, or have direct access to your credit card numbers, banking credentials, or financial details.

4. Data Security

We employ standard technical and administrative security measures to protect the integrity of the application. Since core processing occurs locally on your device, your exposure to privacy risks is minimized.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any modifications will be reflected directly within this page with an updated revision date. You are advised to review this policy periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the app's functionality, please feel free to reach out through our official developer channels or GitHub repository.
